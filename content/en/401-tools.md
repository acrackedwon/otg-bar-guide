## Shakers

**Boston shaker**
A large metal tin with a smaller tin (or pint glass). Fast and easy to clean, which is why most bars use it. It needs a separate strainer.

**Cobbler shaker**
Three pieces with a built-in strainer in the cap. No separate strainer needed, but it jams when it freezes up.

## Mixing glass

For stirred drinks. Thick glass with a wide mouth. Fill it with ice and stir with a bar spoon.

## Strainers

**Hawthorne**
The one with a spring. Used against a shaker tin. This is the strainer you reach for most.

**Julep**
A perforated spoon shape. Used against a mixing glass.

**Fine strainer**
A small mesh sieve held over the glass for double straining.

## Bar spoon

A spoon with a long spiralled handle. Used for stirring, layering and small measures.

## Others

- **Muddler** — for pressing herbs and fruit
- **Peeler / channel knife** — for citrus peel
- **Ice pick** — for shaping ice
- **Squeezer** — for citrus
