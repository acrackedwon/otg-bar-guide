## The rule

| Drink | Glass |
|---|---|
| Chilled, no ice (up) | Coupe / Nick & Nora |
| Over a single large cube | Rocks |
| Long and carbonated | Highball |
| Whisky neat, to be nosed | Glencairn |
| Brandy, cognac, aged rum | Snifter |
| Other spirits neat | Shot glass or small tumbler |
| Sparkling | Flute |

## Preparing the glass

- **Chill stemmed glasses in advance.** Keep them in the freezer, or fill them with ice and water while you build the drink and empty them just before pouring. A room-temperature glass ruins a finished cocktail within minutes.
- The same goes for glasses served over ice.
- **Check the glass against the light** before it goes out.

## Ice

- One **large cube** in a rocks glass. Several small cubes have more surface area and melt faster.
- Fill a highball **to the top**. Less ice actually melts faster.
- Ice absorbs odours. Do not leave the ice machine open.
