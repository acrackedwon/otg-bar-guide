## What it is

The **pre-batched spirit base** for Long Island Iced Tea.
Five spirits in **equal parts**:

![The five bottles used for the pre-mix](/photos/l-i-p-m-5-bottles.jpg)

Left to right in the photo:

| Spirit | Bottle |
|---|---|
| Vodka | The unlabelled bottle — Costco vodka decanted |
| Triple sec | De Kuyper Triple Sec |
| Rum | Barton Light Rum |
| Gin | Barton London Extra Dry Gin |
| Tequila | Durango |

## Which vodka

1. There is a **large bottle of vodka bought at Costco**, usually in the **kitchen under-counter fridge**. Decant from that one.
2. If it is **not there, use Barton vodka**.

![The Costco vodka](/photos/costco-vodka.jpg)

## Making it

**There is no fixed amount. Look at the space you need to fill and divide it by five.**

![The pre-mix bottle — about a third down, so roughly 70ml of each](/photos/l-i-p-m-bottle.jpg)

1. Look at the bottle and judge **how much it needs**.
2. **Divide that by five.** That is how much of each spirit goes in.
3. Measure that amount of each of the five into a beaker.
4. **Stir it enough to combine.**

![Equal measures of all five in the beaker](/photos/l-i-p-m-beaker.jpg)
5. Pour it into the bottle.

### Worked examples

The pre-mix bottle holds **1 litre**.

| Bottle | To fill | Each spirit |
|---|---|---|
| Empty | 1000ml | **200ml** |
| Half left | 500ml | **100ml** |
| A quarter left | 750ml | **150ml** |
| Just topping up | 250ml | **50ml** |

It does not have to be exact. **Eyeball it.**
What matters is not the total but that the **five are in equal amounts**.
