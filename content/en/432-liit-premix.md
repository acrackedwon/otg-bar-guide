## What it is

The **pre-batched spirit base** for Long Island Iced Tea.
Five spirits in **equal parts**:

- Gin
- Vodka
- Rum
- Tequila
- Triple sec

## Which vodka

1. There is a **large bottle of vodka bought at Costco**, usually in the **kitchen under-counter fridge**. Decant from that one.
2. If it is **not there, use Barton vodka**.

## Making it

The pre-mix bottle holds **1 litre**. Five equal parts means **200ml of each**.

1. Measure **200ml of each** of the five into a beaker.
2. **Stir it enough to combine.**
3. Pour it into the pre-mix bottle.

> For a bottle of another size, divide its volume by five.
> (A 750ml bottle takes 150ml of each.)
