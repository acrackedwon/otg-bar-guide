## Version 1 — Boston

- Havana Club 60ml
- Lime 20ml

- Saline solution 2 drops

## Version 2 — Cobbler

- Havana Club 45ml
- Lime 15ml
- Simple syrup 10ml
- Saline solution 1 drop

## Version 3 — Boston

- Havana Club 45ml
- Bacardi 15ml
- Lime 20ml
- Rich syrup 10–13ml
- Saline solution 2 drops

## Version 4 — Boston

- Havana Club 30ml
- Bacardi 22.5ml
- Smith & Cross 7.5ml
- Lime 30ml
- Rich demerara 15ml
- Saline solution 2 drops
