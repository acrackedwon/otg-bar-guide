## Grip

Hold the narrow waist of the jigger **between your index and middle finger**, with your thumb resting lightly underneath. Pinching it with your fingertips makes your hand shake and you spill while pouring.

Held this way you can flip the jigger with a flick of the wrist, so you can switch between the two sizes without regripping.

## The markings

A standard jigger is marked like this.

| Side | Markings |
|---|---|
| Large side | 15 / 30 / **45ml** |
| Small side | 10 / 20 / **30ml** |

The top line is that side's full capacity. For 45ml, fill the large side; for 30ml, either the
middle line on the large side or the small side filled — they are the same measure.

## Measuring

1. **Raise the jigger to eye level** and read the line straight on. Looking down at it gives you less than the full measure.
2. Fill until surface tension makes it bulge slightly. That is the exact measure.
3. Pour **close to** the shaker or mixing glass. Pouring from a height splashes.

## Care

- Rinse whenever you change ingredients. Campari, syrups and anything creamy carry straight into the next drink.
- Citrus juice left in a jigger oxidizes fast. Make rinsing right after use a habit.
- Never replace measuring with eyeballing. It is the single biggest reason the same cocktail tastes different every time.
