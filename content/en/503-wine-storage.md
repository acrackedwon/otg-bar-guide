## Sparkling

It lives in the **kitchen under-counter fridge**, not in the cellars.

## Before serving

Everything is organised inside the cellars. **Open it, check properly**, then serve.
If you cannot find a bottle, do not stand there searching with the door open — it loses its cold. Ask.
