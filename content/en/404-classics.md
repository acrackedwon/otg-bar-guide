> **These are standard specs.** If our house spec differs, say so and it will be corrected here.
> Once the list grows, it reads better split into one entry per drink.

## Negroni

Gin 30 / Campari 30 / Sweet vermouth 30
Stir → rocks glass with a large cube → orange twist

## Martini

Gin 60 / Dry vermouth 10
Stir → chilled Nick & Nora → olive or lemon twist
*Less vermouth if they ask for it dry; 5ml olive brine for dirty*

## Manhattan

Rye 60 / Sweet vermouth 30 / Angostura 2 dashes
Stir → coupe → cherry

## Old Fashioned

Bourbon or rye 60 / 1 tsp sugar (or 7.5 syrup) / Angostura 2 dashes
Build in a rocks glass, large cube, stir → orange twist

## Daiquiri

White rum 60 / Lime juice 22.5 / Simple syrup 15
Shake → double strain → chilled coupe

## Whiskey Sour

Bourbon 45 / Lemon juice 22.5 / Simple syrup 15 / (1 egg white)
With egg white, shake without ice first (dry shake), then again with ice.

## Margarita

Tequila 45 / Triple sec 22.5 / Lime juice 22.5
Shake → rocks glass (ask the guest about a salt rim)

## Gimlet

Gin 60 / Lime juice 22.5 / Simple syrup 15
Shake → chilled coupe

## Mojito

White rum 60 / Lime juice 22.5 / Simple syrup 15 / Mint / Soda
Press the mint gently in the glass for aroma only → rum, lime, syrup → crushed ice → soda → mint sprig

## Gin & Tonic

Gin 45 / Tonic water
Fill a highball with ice → gin → tonic poured gently → one light stir → lime or lemon

## Espresso Martini

Vodka 45 / Coffee liqueur 15 / Espresso 30 / Simple syrup 5
Hard shake → double strain → coupe → three coffee beans

## Every time

- Citrus juice must be **squeezed that day**. Yesterday's tastes completely different.
- Never replace measuring with eyeballing.
- Chill the glass in advance.
- Add the garnish right before it goes out.
