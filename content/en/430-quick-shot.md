## Build

- DeKuyper Cassis 15ml
- Peachtree 15ml
- Ron Diaz 30ml

## Method

In a **Turkish coffee pot** — **light it and throw**.

## Serve

Rocks glass

> This one involves open flame. Clear linen and alcohol away from the area, and keep it turned away from guests.
