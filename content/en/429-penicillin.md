## Build

- Johnnie Walker Black 45ml
- Lemon 20ml
- Ginger syrup 10ml
- Honey 1 bar spoon
- Laphroaig 10 7.5ml — **set aside**

## Method

1. Shake everything **except the Laphroaig**, then pour.
2. **Float** the 7.5ml of Laphroaig 10 on top.

Cobbler shaker.

## Ice

**2 cubes of 4×4**

## Garnish

Lemon twist or ginger candy
