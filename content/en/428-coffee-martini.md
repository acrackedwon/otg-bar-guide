## First — make the coffee

- Maxim coffee powder 15ml
- **Hot** water 30ml

**Dissolve it.**

## Build

- The coffee from above
- Vodka 45ml
- Kahlúa 15ml
- Store-bought syrup 5ml
- Saline solution 2 drops

## Method

Boston shaker with **machine ice**.
**Double strain.**

## Garnish

3 coffee beans
