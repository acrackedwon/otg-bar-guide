## Version 1

- Gordon's 22.5ml
- Campari 22.5ml
- Cinzano Rosso 22.5ml

## Version 2

- Gordon's 40ml
- Campari 25ml
- Cinzano Rosso 20ml

1. Put **3–4 cubes of machine ice** in a rocks glass and work the Campari and Cinzano Rosso together.
2. **Remove the machine ice**, then pour the Gordon's.
3. Add a **5×5 cube** and stir.

## Garnish

Orange twist
