## Build

- Vodka 45ml
- Tomato juice 120ml
- Lemon 10ml
- Salt, pepper
- Tabasco 5 dashes

## Method

**Throw it.** Do not shake — shaking aerates the tomato juice and wrecks the texture.

## Garnish

Lemon wheel, 2 olives
