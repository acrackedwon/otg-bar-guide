## How it is served

**Neat**
A spirit on its own, at room temperature. Poured straight into the glass.

**On the Rocks**
Poured over ice — a rocks glass with a large cube.

**Straight Up / Up**
Chilled first, then served **without ice** in a stemmed glass. Martinis and Daiquiris are served up.
Do not confuse this with neat. Neat is room temperature; up is chilled.

**Straight**
It means without ice. Unlike neat, though, it may also mean they want it **in a straight glass — a shot glass, to drink in one**.

**Always confirm when a guest uses this word.** "In a shot glass, or in a tumbler?" is enough. Guessing gets it wrong half the time.

## How it is made

**Build**
Made directly in the serving glass. Gin & Tonic, highballs.

**Stir**
Stirred with a bar spoon in a mixing glass. For clear, all-spirit drinks.

**Shake**
Shaken in a shaker. Anything with juice, cream or egg white gets shaken.

**Double Strain**
Straining through a fine mesh as well as the strainer, to catch ice shards and herb fragments.
