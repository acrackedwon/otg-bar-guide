## Shaking

### Sealing

1. Put the ingredients in the large tin and fill with ice.
2. Set the small tin in **at a slight angle**. Straight down and it will not release.
3. Tap the top once with your palm to seal.

### Holding

- **One hand under the large tin, the other wrapped over the small tin.**
- Grip across the seam with your fingers. A shaker coming apart mid-shake is an accident.
- Do not rely on your thumb holding the cap alone.

### The shake

- Shake at shoulder height, **out to your side or over your shoulder** — never toward a guest's face.
- Drive the ice straight back and forth between the ends of the tin.
- 10–15 seconds. When frost forms white on the outside of the tin, it is done.

### Opening

Strike the widest point of the seam with the heel of your hand. Do not pull it apart by force.

## Stirring

1. Fill the mixing glass **completely** with ice. Too little ice melts fast and waters the drink down.
2. Slide the bar spoon in with its back against the glass.
3. Hold the handle between thumb and index finger and let the spoon **slide between your middle and ring finger**. The fingers turn it, not the arm.
4. The ice should rotate quietly as one mass. Rattling means you are doing it wrong.
5. 20–30 seconds. Frost on the outside of the glass means it is done.

## Which one

- **All-spirit**, clear drinks → stir (Martini, Manhattan, Negroni)
- Anything with **juice, cream, egg white or syrup** → shake (Daiquiri, sours, Margarita)

Shaking a clear drink aerates it and turns it cloudy.
