## Why we use it

Now and then a guest asks for a cocktail with lime, but not often enough for us to keep limes in stock.
So we use **pseudo juice in place of lime juice**.

It is made **about once every two weeks**.

## Making it

1. Give the limes a proper rinse.
2. **Peel off all the skin** with a knife or peeler.
   Take **only the green layer** and as little white as possible. Cut away any white pith left on — it turns the juice bitter.
3. **Weigh the peel.**
4. Open Steve the Bartender's [pseudo juice calculator](https://app.stevethebartender.com.au/calc/pseudo-juice).
   (Searching "Pseudo Juice Calculator" finds it too.)
5. Choose **lime** and enter the **peel weight**. It tells you how much of each ingredient to add.
6. Put the peel in a container with the calculated amounts of **sugar, citric acid, malic acid and salt**.
7. **Cover with cling film and leave for 2–3 hours.**
8. By then the peel has lost its colour and the sugar has dissolved into liquid.
9. Measure out the calculated amount of **still water** and put everything into the blender.
   - Use that water to **rinse out the container**, scraping every bit of sugar, powder and peel into the blender. Anything left behind throws the ratio off.
10. Blend for **1–2 minutes**.
11. **Strain through cheesecloth.**
12. Bottle only the strained liquid and **label it**.

## Keeping it

> **After two weeks, throw it out and make a new batch.**
> That is why **writing the date it was made on the label matters.** Without a date, nobody knows when it is due to go.
