## Why

Imported citrus is **coated in wax and treated for shelf life**. As long as we use the peel, it cannot go straight into a drink.

## Washing

1. Rinse under running water.
2. **Scrub with baking soda.** You can feel the wax come off the surface.
3. Or soak them in **hot water (40–50°C) for 30 seconds** and then scrub. The wax softens and lifts.
4. Rinse thoroughly.
5. **Dry completely.** Stored damp, they mould quickly. Skipping this step undoes the washing.

## Storing

- Refrigerate once fully dry.
- Store them so **air can move** rather than sealed airtight. Sealed containers trap moisture and they spoil.
- One spoiled fruit spreads fast to the ones next to it. Pull it out as soon as you see it.
- It helps to separate the ones for peel from the ones for juice.

## Juice

- **Squeeze it the day you use it.** Lemon and lime juice change noticeably within hours.
- Roll them under your palm before squeezing to get more juice.
- Fruit at room temperature squeezes better than cold.
