## Why

Imported citrus is **coated in wax and treated for shelf life**. As long as we use the peel, it cannot go straight into a drink.

## The steps

1. **Peel off every sticker** on the new lemons.
2. **Soak them in lukewarm water** for a first wash.
3. Pour that water away. Fill again with lukewarm water, **sprinkle in baking soda and let it dissolve, then soak them.**
4. After a while, pour the water away and **wipe each lemon clean under water, one by one.**
5. **Dry every one thoroughly with a cloth** before storing.

## Storing

- Store them only once **completely dry**. Damp, they mould quickly — skipping step 5 undoes everything before it.
- One spoiled fruit spreads fast to the ones next to it. Pull it out as soon as you see it.
