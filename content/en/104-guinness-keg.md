## Watch the video first

The video above is the basic sequence. Watching it once beats reading about it.

## While you do it

- **Shut the gas off first.** Close the gas valve before you disconnect the coupler.
  Touching a coupler still under pressure makes it fly up at you.
- To remove the coupler, **pull the handle up to release the lock, then turn** it off.
- **Wipe the coupler seat on the new keg** before connecting.
- **Open the gas valve again** once connected. Forget this and it pours a few glasses, then stops.
- The first pour after a change comes out very foamy. **Write off one glass** and keep pouring until it settles.
- Move the empty keg out and note it for the next order.

## Care

- Kegs are heavy. **Lift with your legs, not your back.** Do not struggle alone — ask for help.
- The gas cylinder is a **pressure vessel**. Keep it secured upright and do not knock the valve.
