## Build

**A**
- Hennessy 45ml
- Lemon 15ml
- Cointreau 15ml
- Rich demerara 1 bar spoon

**B**
- Hennessy 45ml
- Lemon 15ml
- Cointreau 10ml
- Grand Marnier 5ml

## Method

Cobbler shaker.

> **Do not over-chill this one.** Too cold and the aroma closes up.
