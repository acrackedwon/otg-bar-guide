## What we use

Apple, orange, chamoe (Korean melon), pear.

## Apple

**Cut into wedges, skin left on.**

Quarter it, remove the core, then cut into wedges. Do not peel it.

## Orange

**Wedges, served with the peel on.**

## Chamoe (Korean melon)

A small yellow melon with white flesh, eaten firm and mildly sweet.

- **Do not remove the seeds.** They stay in.
- **Peel it with a peeler**, not a knife.

## Pear

Peel it and cut into wedges.

## Working with the knife

- **Put a damp cloth under the board** so it cannot slide.
- Curl the fingers of your guide hand under and rest the blade against your knuckles.
- **Cut one flat side first** on anything round, then lay it on that face.
- Never catch a falling knife. Step back.
- A dull knife is more dangerous than a sharp one. It slips.

## Plating

- Cut anything that browns quickly (apple, pear) **to order**. Never prep it ahead.
- Send it out with forks or picks.
