## Safety

- **Put a damp cloth under the board** so it cannot slide.
- Curl the fingers of your guide hand under and rest the blade against your knuckles.
- **Cut one flat side first** on anything round, then lay it on that face. Cutting something that can roll is how people get hurt.
- Never catch a falling knife. Step back.
- A dull knife is more dangerous than a sharp one. It slips.

## The basics

**Apple / Pear**
Quarter → remove the core → peel and cut into wedges. A brief soak in salted or lemon water slows browning.

**Orange / Grapefruit**
Cut off top and bottom → stand it up and cut the peel away down to the flesh → cut between the membranes to release the segments.

**Pineapple**
Top and bottom off → stand and peel → cut out the remaining eyes in a V groove → quarter and remove the core → bite-sized pieces.

**Melon**
Halve and seed → cut into wedges → run the knife between skin and flesh.

**Grapes / Berries**
Rinse under running water and **dry completely**. Water left on them pools in the dish.

## Plating

- Keep colours from sitting next to each other.
- Cut anything that browns quickly (apple, pear, banana) **to order**. Never prep it ahead.
- Send it out with forks or picks.
