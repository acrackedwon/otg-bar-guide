## Bourbon

- Maker's Mark 55ml
- Angostura 4 dashes
- Rich demerara 1 bar spoon (a touch generous)

Stir **30–40 rotations**.

## Rye

- Rye **30ml first**, the remaining 20ml later
- Angostura 4 dashes
- Rich demerara 1 bar spoon (a touch generous)

1. Stir the first 30ml of rye with the bitters and syrup.
   - If the bottle is kept **refrigerated**: **45+ rotations**
   - If it is at **room temperature**: **30+ rotations**
2. **Add the remaining 20ml** of rye and keep stirring, judging by eye.

> The rye goes in in two pours, never all at once.
